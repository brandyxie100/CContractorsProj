# Product Brief

## Problem

Clements Contractors owns and deploys trucks, diggers, and quarry equipment across multiple job sites. Today, availability is typically tracked with spreadsheets, whiteboards, or phone calls — leading to double-booking, idle machines, and crews waiting on equipment.

## Product distinction: contractor vs rental company

Most equipment software (EZRentOut, Point of Rental, RentalMan) is built for **rental companies** that invoice third-party customers. Clements is a **contractor** that owns its fleet and assigns it to its own projects.

| Concern | Rental company | Contractor (us) |
|---------|----------------|-----------------|
| Primary object | Customer / contract | Project / job |
| Revenue model | Rental billing | Utilization & dispatch efficiency |
| Invoicing | Required | Not in scope |
| Telematics / hours | Helpful | Essential for heavy plant |
| Smart search | Nice-to-have | Differentiator (Smart Dispatch Finder) |

## Target customer

- **Company**: Clements Contractors (earthworks & contracting, 28+ years)
- **Assets**: Quarries, truck fleet, excavators and related plant
- **Users**: Dispatchers, project managers, equipment managers, owners (read)

## Pain points addressed

1. Fleet visibility across sites
2. Scheduling conflicts / double-booking
3. Maintenance coordination without disrupting jobs
4. Utilization tracking (buy vs rent decisions)
5. Finding the nearest available machine quickly

## Reference systems

| System | Role |
|--------|------|
| Clue | Primary — contractor workflow, telematics-first |
| EZRentOut | UI — availability calendar pattern |
| RentalMan IntelliSource | Differentiator — smart “find a machine” search |

## Phased roadmap

### Phase 1 — MVP (core availability)

Asset registry, availability calendar, assignments, projects, status tracking, next-available date, filtering, user roles.

### Phase 2 — Smart ops

Smart Dispatch Finder, alternative suggestions, maintenance scheduling (blocks availability), utilization reports, mobile-responsive UI, location/distance, notifications (stretch).

### Phase 3 — Advanced

Telematics, QR check-in/out, cost tracking, bundles, external APIs, multi-branch.

## Out of scope (MVP–Phase 2)

- Rental billing / invoicing
- Public customer portal
- Multi-company tenancy
